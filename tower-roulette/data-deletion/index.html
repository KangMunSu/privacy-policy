Account and data deletion

Delete in the App

  1. Open Settings → Account.
  2. Sign in to Google Play Games if needed.
  3. Select Delete cloud save.
  4. Read the confirmation and choose Yes. Choose No to cancel.

Request without the App

Email kangmunsu.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Tower Roulette data deletion request.” Identify the App and follow our instructions to provide only the minimum information needed to verify ownership. If you cannot access the App or device, explain that so we can help verify your request. Never send passwords, one-time authorization codes or payment details.

Scope and retained information

We delete Tower Roulette cloud progress and login sessions linked to your player-ID hash from our server. This does not delete your Google Account, Play Games profile, other apps' data or local progress. Android app-data settings can remove local saves. Signing in again may upload any remaining local progress. Google-managed game/profile data can be managed separately in Play Games settings.

Deleting cloud gameplay does not delete Google Play orders or actual purchase entitlements. Minimum purchase-verification, security and operational records may be retained for disputes, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Provider retention and deletion policies apply to information processed directly by Google, RevenueCat and AWS.

Leaderboards and private battle history (2026-10-27)

Confirm cloud-save deletion in Settings, or request deletion of server game data by email after ownership verification, to delete cloud progress, sessions, leaderboard data and private battle history. The account’s private on-device cache and pending result queue are also cleared. This does not delete general local game progress, the Google account, Play Games profile or actual purchase entitlements. Signing in and playing again starts new records.

Leaderboard data and private battle history are retained until a deletion request to provide the service and prevent duplicate counting. Transfers use TLS and private records are restricted to the authenticated account. Encrypted automated RDS backups are retained for seven days, so copies made before deletion may remain until expiry and are not used for ordinary queries.

Le cache privé local et la file de résultats du compte sont également effacés. La progression locale générale, le compte Google, le profil Play Games et les droits d’achat ne sont pas supprimés. Une nouvelle connexion suivie de parties crée de nouveaux enregistrements.

Le classement et l’historique privé sont conservés jusqu’à une demande de suppression pour fournir le service et éviter les doublons. TLS protège les transferts et l’accès privé est limité au compte authentifié.
