Account and data deletion

Haru Studio games · Tower Roulette · com.gmsgm.towerroulette

Delete in the App

  1. Open Settings → Account.
  2. Sign in to Google Play Games if needed.
  3. Select Delete cloud save.
  4. Read the confirmation and choose Yes. Choose No to cancel.

Request without the App

Email kangmunsu.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Tower Roulette data deletion request.” Identify the App and follow our instructions to provide only the minimum information needed to verify ownership. If you cannot access the App or device, explain that so we can help verify your request. Never send passwords, one-time authorization codes or payment details.

Scope and retained information

We delete Tower Roulette cloud progress and login sessions linked to your player-ID hash from our server. This does not delete your Google Account, Play Games profile, other apps' data or local progress. Android app-data settings can remove local saves. Signing in again may upload any remaining local progress. Google-managed game/profile data can be managed separately in Play Games settings.

Deleting cloud gameplay does not delete Google Play orders or actual purchase entitlements. Minimum purchase-verification, security and operational records may be retained for disputes, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Provider retention and deletion policies apply to information processed directly by Google, Unity and AWS.
