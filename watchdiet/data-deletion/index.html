English

WatchDiet has no account. To delete an individual meal or weight entry, open it in the app and choose Delete. To delete all records and saved photos on a device, clear WatchDiet storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. This cannot be undone; Android automatic backup is disabled.

Our analysis server does not store meal records or photos. Anonymous request and usage metadata is automatically removed after about two UTC days; photo-analysis consent history is kept for 90 days, and credentials expire after 30 days before later cleanup. To ask us to review or delete server metadata earlier, email kangmunsu.dev@gmail.com with the subject [WatchDiet] Data Deletion. Describe the approximate analysis date and your request. Do not send photos, credentials or passwords. Because the app has no account or email linked to the anonymous installation, we may need additional non-secret information to locate and verify a matching record; we will explain if a specific record cannot be identified. OpenAI controls data processed on its systems under its own policy.

No envíes fotos, credenciales ni contraseñas. Al no haber cuenta, puede que no podamos identificar un registro concreto; te lo explicaremos.
